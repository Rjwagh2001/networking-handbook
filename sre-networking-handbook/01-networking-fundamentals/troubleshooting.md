# Networking Fundamentals — Troubleshooting Notes

Add validated troubleshooting scenarios as they are practiced.

## Scenario template

### Symptom

What did the user or monitoring system report?

### Scope and impact

Which host, service, subnet or users are affected?

### Investigation

Record commands, timestamps and sanitized outputs. Explain why each check was performed.

### Findings

What evidence narrowed down the failure?

### Root cause

State the cause supported by the evidence. Do not guess.

### Mitigation

What restored service safely?

### Permanent corrective action

What prevents recurrence?

### Verification

How was recovery confirmed?
